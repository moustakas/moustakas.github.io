Welcome! I am a Professor in the [Department of Physics &
Astronomy](https://www.siena.edu/departments/physics-and-astronomy/)
at [Siena University](https://www.siena.edu/), where I serve as the
[4+1 Engineering
Co-Coordinator](https://www.siena.edu/programs/engineering) and on the
[Data Science Faculty Steering
Committee](https://www.siena.edu/programs/data-science/faculty-steering-committee).

My [research](/research/) is in observational cosmology and galaxy
evolution. I use the largest spectroscopic and imaging surveys ever
undertaken to understand how galaxies form and evolve in a universe
dominated by dark matter, and to measure the properties of dark
energy, the mysterious phenomenon causing the expansion of the
universe to accelerate.

I am a longtime member of the [Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument
(DESI)](https://www.desi.lbl.gov/) collaboration and a core team
member of the [DESI Legacy Imaging
Surveys](https://www.legacysurvey.org/), and I lead several
[open-source software](/software/) and [data
projects](/research/surveys-and-data-releases/), including the [Siena
Galaxy Atlas](https://sga.readthedocs.io/) and
[FastSpecFit](https://fastspecfit.readthedocs.io/).

Much of my work relies on the computational and statistical methods of
data science, which Siena undergraduates are an essential part of; see
my [research group](/group/).

I [teach](/teaching/) a wide range of courses, from introductory
physics to upper-level physics and astrophysics and data science, and
I strive to build every class around the teaching methods that physics
education research has shown to work best.

If you would like to know more, please do not hesitate to [get in
touch](/contact/)!
