I work on a wide range of problems in observational cosmology and galaxy evolution, much of it built on the largest spectroscopic and imaging surveys of the sky. Most of my current work centers on the [Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI)](https://www.desi.lbl.gov/) and the [DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys](https://www.legacysurvey.org/).
