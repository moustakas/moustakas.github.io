I am the primary developer of several open-source software packages and have contributed to the pipelines behind the DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys and DESI. For the data products built with them, see [Surveys and Data Releases](/research/surveys-and-data-releases/).

## Siena Galaxy Atlas (SGA)

{{< img src="software/sga2020-montage.jpg" alt="Montage of 42 color images of nearby galaxies from the Siena Galaxy Atlas" caption="A sample of galaxies from the Siena Galaxy Atlas 2020." >}}

The pipeline that builds the Siena Galaxy Atlas, a multiwavelength atlas of large, nearby galaxies, from the DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys.

- Documentation: [sga.readthedocs.io](https://sga.readthedocs.io/)
- Code: [github.com/moustakas/SGA](https://github.com/moustakas/SGA)

## FastSpecFit

{{< img src="software/fastspecfit-example-bgs.png" alt="FastSpecFit model of a DESI Bright Galaxy Survey target at redshift 0.0813: the broadband spectral energy distribution with photometry and a color image of the galaxy, the full DESI spectrum with its best-fitting model, and zoomed-in fits to four groups of emission lines." caption="FastSpecFit model of a DESI Bright Galaxy Survey galaxy at *z*=0.0813: the ultraviolet-to-infrared spectral energy distribution (top), the DESI spectrum (middle), and fits to the strongest emission lines (bottom)." >}}

Fast spectral synthesis and emission-line fitting of DESI spectra and photometry (2021–present).

- Documentation: [fastspecfit.readthedocs.io](https://fastspecfit.readthedocs.io/)
- Code: [github.com/desihub/fastspecfit](https://github.com/desihub/fastspecfit)
- Software record: [FastSpecFit: Fast spectral synthesis and emission-line fitting of DESI spectra](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023ascl.soft08005M/abstract), Astrophysics Source Code Library, ascl:2308.005

## Other Software Contributions

- [legacypipe](https://github.com/legacysurvey/legacypipe/): image reduction and photometry pipeline for the DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys ([ascl:2502.024](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025ascl.soft02024L/abstract))
- [desitarget](https://github.com/desihub/desitarget): selection of DESI targets from photometric catalogs ([ascl:2301.025](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023ascl.soft01025M/abstract))
- [desispec](https://github.com/desihub/desispec): the DESI spectroscopic data-reduction pipeline
- [desisim](https://github.com/desihub/desisim): simulations of DESI spectra and data
